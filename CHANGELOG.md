## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

First public release.

- Records both sides of any call (your mic + the call's audio) and transcribes on-device with Whisper
- Dynamic Island around the MacBook notch, or a floating Liquid Glass bubble on other Macs
- Live transcript laid out like Messages; summary, decisions and action items by Apple Intelligence
- Speaker notes with a teleprompter and live-updating linked docs (Google Docs, GitHub, any page)
- AI Assist: answers from Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, plus Ask about the meeting on-device
- Hidden from screen sharing; nothing leaves your Mac
- Light at rest: the speech engine loads only during meetings
