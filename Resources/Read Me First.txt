Hoot — private meeting notes for your Mac
=========================================

Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later.

INSTALL
1. Drag Hoot into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Hoot from Applications.
   If macOS says it "could not verify" Hoot: click Done, then open
   System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
   (Only once. It happens because Hoot isn't from the App Store.)

FIRST LAUNCH
• Hoot opens a short Setup checklist: allow the Microphone and
  "Screen & System Audio Recording" (so it can hear the other people),
  then reopen Hoot. The speech model (about 550 MB) downloads once.
• On a MacBook with a notch, Hoot lives in the notch like a Dynamic Island.
  Point at the notch to open it. Other Macs get a small floating bubble.

USING IT
• Click Record in the notch (or the bird in the bubble), or press Control-Option-R.
• When you stop, Hoot saves the transcript and — with Apple Intelligence on —
  writes a summary with decisions and action items.
• Notes are saved in Documents › Meeting Notes. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
• Hoot is hidden from screen sharing — only you can see it.
• Settings: right-click Hoot › Settings…  (or ⌘, when Hoot's window is open)

Updates: Hoot checks for new versions once a day and tells you in its menu.
Website: https://sagar-darji.github.io/hoot/

Be kind: let people know when you're recording a conversation.
